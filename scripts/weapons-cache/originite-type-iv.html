How to Get Originite: Type IV

Pull From Any Convene Banners

Convene Banners
Through Verses and Rhythms Anniversary Resonator Convene Phase 2
Absolute Pulsation (Woodland Aria) Anniversary Weapon Convene Phase 2
Tidal Chorus Utterance of Marvels
Beginner's Choice Convene Banner Weapon Permanent Convene

The Originite: Type IV is obtained from any active Convene Banners in Wuthering Waves!

Obtained from Chests

The Originite: Type IV is obtainable from chests scattered all over the vast open world of Wuthering Waves. Make sure to open each one you see to get multiple copies of this weapon.
